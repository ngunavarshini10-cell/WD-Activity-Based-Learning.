Library Rules & Regulations

Help us maintain a peaceful learning environment.

01

Borrowing Period

Students can borrow up to 3 books for a period of 14 days.

02

Return on Time

Return books on or before the due date to avoid late fees.

03

Keep Books Safe

Do not damage, mark, fold or write inside library books.

04

Maintain Silence

Keep mobile phones silent and maintain a quiet atmosphere.
